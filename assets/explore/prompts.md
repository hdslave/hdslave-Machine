## emblem-atlas

A single production sprite atlas on solid pure black. EXACT 3 columns by 3 rows of 9 distinct centered emblems occupying uniform square tiles. No text, marks, labels or rules. All equal size, with generous margins. Original late-90s acid-flyer machine sculptures, three-quarter axonometric, bold silver-violet metallic facets with turquoise reflected edge, cool orchid shadows and black cutouts. Keep extremely simple, 2–4 chunky planes per icon, punchy, recognizable as 28px bitmaps. Ordered: top-left folded split torus knot (FM); top-middle bass impact cone with hole; top-right twin tensioned snare discs with two crisscross wires. Middle-left three staggered crescent clap fins; middle-middle six-bladed thin hat petals opening upward; middle-right a flared bell with a short nub. Bottom-left a square helical coil of stacked angled metal plates; bottom-middle an isolated dark sphere with three grounding feet; bottom-right a distinctive rectangular cartridge with 8 recessed square pads (2x4). The shapes are original geometric instruments, a single coherent family. Industrial print, sculpted silhouettes sharp and legible, no panels or visuals in gaps.

## phase-band

A wide 3:1 abstract panoramic texture for a tiny synthesizer marquee: black ground and one sweeping folded magnetic ribbon only, crescendo from low left through center then curling upper right. Nested offset violet contour lines, like an original metallic folded film twisting in vacuum. Violet and indigo inks, occasional dusky lilac cut edge at its crest, very dark. Elegant simple optical screenprint, two broad readable folds, ample pure black negative space. Edge-to-edge trim. No text, logos, stars, frames, labels or other objects. Designed to read at 90x28 pixels.
