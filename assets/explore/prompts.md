## FM TONE phase slip

Design one original, edgy acid-graphics sound emblem for a synthesizer, readable at 30 pixels. Subject: a digital eclipse interrupted by a hard phase slip. Begin with ONE chunky hollow elliptical halo leaning forward about thirty degrees. Slash it cleanly across the middle on a shallow rising diagonal, then displace the severed center slice markedly to the right. Two open arc fragments and a single sharply clipped, offset bridge of violet material. The result is an asymmetric broken optical stencil, like a misregistered ring cut from a late-'90s rave pressing plate. Big angular ends on the cuts, one black aperture, two broad slashes of negative space. The left and right parts must feel under shearing tension, with a bold dynamic contour. Very limited ink: near-flat pale lavender on the cut upper planes, deep amethyst underside, a short icy cyan rim exactly as in the supplied material sample. Contemporary and spare. Design the material itself, zero decorative micro-detail. Entire emblem centered alone on pure black, comfortably inset in square frame, near-square strong silhouette. No doubled holes, infinity symbol, onion or organic ridges, leaves, waves, spheres, planets, targets, concentric rings, lightning bolts, letters, text, arrows, logo badge, horizontal speed lines or surrounding effects. The reference illustrates ONLY the cool enamel palette; invent the silhouette independently.
